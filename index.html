How it works

Pick a class and a number of moves. The page shows a DR case as a scramble and as a cube you can drag around. Find a solution that reaches DR, then press Found it to stop the clock and check it against the optimal ones.

Spaced review shows cases that are due, then new ones, and brings a missed case back within a minute. Recap shows every case in the set exactly once.

Cases are every DR with an optimal length up to 6, one per shape. Mirrors and turns of U and D before the case count as the same case.
